Graduate thesis

Stabilization of dispersive soil using EICP (Enzyme Induced Carbonate Precipitation) and Biopolymer

M.Sc. Thesis · BUET

The study aims to develop a sustainable technique for stabilizing dispersive soils using Enzyme-Induced Carbonate Precipitation (EICP) combined with biopolymers. It focuses on improving soil strength, reducing dispersion potential, and enhancing durability through eco-friendly bio-mediated treatment methods.

Undergraduate thesis

Experimental and Numerical Investigation of Bearing Capacity of Circular Footing on Clay Soil

B.Sc. Thesis · BUET

The study aimed to investigate the bearing behavior of circular footings on clay through laboratory experiments and to validate the findings using a numerical approach based on the finite element method in PLAXIS 3D software. The experimental setup was designed considering boundary effects. The research analyzed the load–settlement response and examined the relationship between settlement and footing diameter, providing a comparative evaluation between experimental and numerical results.

Supervisor: Dr. Mohammed Kabirul Islam, Professor, Dept. of Civil Engineering, BUET
